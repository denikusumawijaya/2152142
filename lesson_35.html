🔥 Pemanasan: Review Spiral Spiral Review Warm-up

Sebelum belajar rumus jarak, kita perlu menguasai lima keterampilan dasar: mengkuadratkan bilangan (termasuk bilangan negatif), mencari akar kuadrat, menyederhanakan bentuk akar, menghitung selisih bilangan bulat, dan membaca koordinat Kartesius.Before learning the distance formula, we need to master five basic skills: squaring numbers (including negative numbers), finding square roots, simplifying surds, finding the difference of integers, and reading Cartesian coordinates.

A. Kuadrat Bilangan A. Squaring Numbers

Kuadrat suatu bilangan artinya bilangan itu dikalikan dengan dirinya sendiri.The square of a number means the number multiplied by itself.

\( a^2 = a \times a \)

Ingat aturan tanda: negatif × negatif = positif. Jadi kuadrat bilangan apa pun tidak pernah negatif.Remember the sign rule: negative × negative = positive. So the square of any number is never negative.

Contoh / Example
\[ (-7)^2 = (-7)\times(-7) = 49 \qquad (-4)^2 = (-4)\times(-4)=16 \]
⚠️ Hati-hati! \((-5)^2\) berbeda dengan \(-5^2\). Tanda kurung menentukan apa yang dikuadratkan.Careful! \((-5)^2\) is different from \(-5^2\). The brackets decide what gets squared. \[ (-5)^2 = 25 \qquad\text{tetapi / but}\qquad -5^2 = -(5\times5) = -25 \] Kesalahan umum lain: \((-6)^2 \neq 12\). Mengkuadratkan bukan mengalikan dengan 2.Another common mistake: \((-6)^2 \neq 12\). Squaring is not multiplying by 2.

B. Akar Kuadrat & Bilangan Kuadrat Sempurna B. Square Roots & Perfect Squares

Akar kuadrat adalah kebalikan dari kuadrat. \(\sqrt{n}=m\) artinya \(m^2=n\) (dengan \(m \ge 0\)). Bilangan seperti 49 dan 64 disebut bilangan kuadrat sempurna karena akarnya bilangan bulat.The square root is the inverse of squaring. \(\sqrt{n}=m\) means \(m^2=n\) (with \(m \ge 0\)). Numbers such as 49 and 64 are called perfect squares because their roots are whole numbers.

\(1^2=1\)
\(2^2=4\)
\(3^2=9\)
\(4^2=16\)
\(5^2=25\)
\(6^2=36\)
\(7^2=49\)
\(8^2=64\)
\(9^2=81\)
\(10^2=100\)
\(11^2=121\)
\(12^2=144\)
\(13^2=169\)
\(14^2=196\)
\(15^2=225\)
Contoh / Example
Tentukan \(\sqrt{169}\). Cari bilangan yang jika dikuadratkan menghasilkan 169.Find \(\sqrt{169}\). Look for the number whose square is 169. \[ 13^2 = 169 \;\Rightarrow\; \sqrt{169} = 13 \] Bilangan 50 bukan kuadrat sempurna karena berada di antara \(7^2=49\) dan \(8^2=64\).The number 50 is not a perfect square because it lies between \(7^2=49\) and \(8^2=64\).

C. Menyederhanakan Bentuk Akar C. Simplifying Surds

Jika hasil akar bukan bilangan bulat, sederhanakan dengan sifat berikut:If the root is not a whole number, simplify it using this property:

\( \sqrt{a \times b} = \sqrt{a}\times\sqrt{b} \)
  1. Cari faktor kuadrat sempurna terbesar dari bilangan di dalam akar.Find the largest perfect-square factor of the number under the root.
  2. Tulis bilangan sebagai perkalian (kuadrat sempurna) × (sisa).Write the number as (perfect square) × (the rest).
  3. Akarkan bagian kuadrat sempurna, keluarkan dari tanda akar.Take the root of the perfect square and move it outside the root sign.
Contoh / Example
\[ \sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36\times 2} = \sqrt{36}\times\sqrt{2} = 6\sqrt{2} \] \[ \sqrt{20} = \sqrt{4\times 5} = 2\sqrt{5} \qquad \sqrt{32}=\sqrt{16\times2}=4\sqrt2 \]

D. Selisih Bilangan Bulat D. Difference of Integers

Pada rumus jarak kita sering mengurangkan bilangan negatif. Ingat: mengurangi bilangan negatif sama dengan menambah bilangan positif.In the distance formula we often subtract negative numbers. Remember: subtracting a negative number is the same as adding a positive number.

\( a-(-b) = a+b \)
Contoh / Example
\[ 3-(-4) = 3+4 = 7 \qquad -2-5 = -7 \qquad -1-(-6) = -1+6 = 5 \]

E. Membaca Koordinat Kartesius E. Reading Cartesian Coordinates

Titik ditulis \((x,y)\). Nilai \(x\) (absis) = geser mendatar dari titik asal O: kanan (+), kiri (−). Nilai \(y\) (ordinat) = geser tegak: atas (+), bawah (−).A point is written \((x,y)\). The \(x\)-value (abscissa) = horizontal move from origin O: right (+), left (−). The \(y\)-value (ordinate) = vertical move: up (+), down (−).

Contoh / Example
\(A(2,3)\): kanan 2, atas 3. \(B(-4,1)\): kiri 4, atas 1. \(C(-2,-3)\): kiri 2, bawah 3. \(D(3,-2)\): kanan 3, bawah 2.\(A(2,3)\): right 2, up 3. \(B(-4,1)\): left 4, up 1. \(C(-2,-3)\): left 2, down 3. \(D(3,-2)\): right 3, down 2.

💡 Tips: selalu baca \(x\) dulu, baru \(y\) — "jalan dulu, baru naik/turun lift".💡 Tip: always read \(x\) first, then \(y\) — "walk first, then take the lift up/down".

🌍 Pengantar & Kaitan Dunia Nyata Introduction & Real-World Connection

Pertanyaan besar hari ini: Bagaimana cara menghitung jarak garis lurus antara dua titik pada bidang koordinat?Today's big question: How do we calculate the straight-line distance between two points on a coordinate plane?

🗺️ Cerita: Rumah Dina ke Sekolah Story: From Dina's House to School

Peta kota berbentuk grid. Setiap kotak = 1 blok. Rumah Dina 🏠 di \((1,1)\) dan sekolah 🏫 di \((5,4)\).The city map is a grid. Each square = 1 block. Dina's house 🏠 is at \((1,1)\) and the school 🏫 is at \((5,4)\).

🟧 Dina berjalan kaki menyusuri jalan: ke timur lalu ke utara.🟧 Dina walks along the streets: east, then north.

\[ \text{mendatar/horizontal} = 5-1 = 4 \text{ blok} \] \[ \text{tegak/vertical} = 4-1 = 3 \text{ blok} \] \[ \text{jalan kaki/walking} = 4+3 = 7 \text{ blok} \]

🟦 Drone terbang lurus dari rumah ke sekolah. Lintasannya menjadi sisi miring segitiga siku-siku dengan sisi tegak 4 dan 3:🟦 A drone flies straight from the house to the school. Its path is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 4 and 3:

\[ \text{drone} = \sqrt{4^2+3^2} = \sqrt{16+9} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \text{ blok} \]

Kesimpulan: jarak garis lurus (5 blok) lebih pendek daripada jarak menyusuri jalan grid (7 blok). Untuk menghitung jarak garis lurus, kita memakai Teorema Pythagoras.Conclusion: the straight-line distance (5 blocks) is shorter than the distance along the grid streets (7 blocks). To find a straight-line distance we use the Pythagorean Theorem.

Jenis jarak Type of distanceCara menghitung How to calculateContoh Dina Dina's example
Menyusuri jalan grid Along grid streets\(\text{mendatar}+\text{tegak}\) horizontal + vertical\(4+3=7\)
Garis lurus Straight line\(\sqrt{\text{mendatar}^2+\text{tegak}^2}\) √(horizontal² + vertical²)\(\sqrt{16+9}=5\)
Kasus khusus / Special case
Jika dua tempat berada pada garis mendatar yang sama (nilai \(y\) sama), jaraknya cukup selisih nilai \(x\). Contoh: \((2,3)\) dan \((8,3)\) berjarak \(8-2=6\) blok. Begitu juga jika nilai \(x\) sama, jaraknya = selisih nilai \(y\).If two places are on the same horizontal line (same \(y\)-value), the distance is simply the difference of the \(x\)-values. Example: \((2,3)\) and \((8,3)\) are \(8-2=6\) blocks apart. Likewise, if the \(x\)-values are equal, the distance = difference of the \(y\)-values.

🔁 Mengulas Pengetahuan Awal Review of Prior Knowledge

Rumus jarak dibangun dari Teorema Pythagoras. Mari kita ulas kembali teorema ini.The distance formula is built on the Pythagorean Theorem. Let's review the theorem.

📐 Teorema Pythagoras The Pythagorean Theorem

Pada segitiga siku-siku, sisi di depan sudut siku-siku disebut sisi miring (hipotenusa) \(c\) — sisi terpanjang. Dua sisi lain \(a\) dan \(b\) disebut sisi siku-siku.In a right triangle, the side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse \(c\) — the longest side. The other two sides \(a\) and \(b\) are the legs.

\( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \)

Bentuk lain:Other forms:

\[ c=\sqrt{a^2+b^2} \qquad a=\sqrt{c^2-b^2} \qquad b=\sqrt{c^2-a^2} \]

🧮 Contoh Soal & Langkah Worked Examples & Steps

Langkah mencari sisi miring:Steps to find the hypotenuse:

  1. Identifikasi kedua sisi siku-siku \(a\) dan \(b\).Identify the two legs \(a\) and \(b\).
  2. Kuadratkan masing-masing sisi siku-siku.Square each leg.
  3. Jumlahkan kedua hasil kuadrat.Add the two squares.
  4. Tarik akar kuadrat dari jumlahnya.Take the square root of the sum.
Contoh 1 — mencari sisi miring / Example 1 — finding the hypotenuse
Sisi siku-siku 6 cm dan 8 cm.The legs are 6 cm and 8 cm. \[ c=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=\sqrt{100}=10 \text{ cm} \]
Contoh 2 — mencari sisi siku-siku / Example 2 — finding a leg
Sisi miring 13 cm, satu sisi siku-siku 5 cm. Gunakan pengurangan, bukan penjumlahan.Hypotenuse 13 cm, one leg 5 cm. Use subtraction, not addition. \[ b=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{169-25}=\sqrt{144}=12 \text{ cm} \]
Contoh 3 — hasil bentuk akar / Example 3 — surd answer
\[ c=\sqrt{2^2+3^2}=\sqrt{4+9}=\sqrt{13}\approx 3{,}61 \]
Contoh 4 — kebalikan teorema / Example 4 — the converse
Untuk mengecek apakah segitiga siku-siku: bandingkan (jumlah kuadrat dua sisi terpendek) dengan (kuadrat sisi terpanjang).To check if a triangle is right-angled: compare (sum of squares of the two shorter sides) with (square of the longest side). \[ 5,12,13:\; 5^2+12^2=25+144=169=13^2 \;\checkmark \text{ siku-siku / right} \] \[ 4,5,6:\; 4^2+5^2=16+25=41 \neq 36=6^2 \;\times \text{ bukan siku-siku / not right} \]

🔢 Tripel Pythagoras Pythagorean Triples

Tiga bilangan bulat \(a,b,c\) yang memenuhi \(a^2+b^2=c^2\). Kelipatannya juga tripel (misal \(3,4,5 \to 6,8,10 \to 9,12,15\)). Menghafal tripel membuat hitungan jarak jauh lebih cepat!Three whole numbers \(a,b,c\) satisfying \(a^2+b^2=c^2\). Their multiples are triples too (e.g. \(3,4,5 \to 6,8,10 \to 9,12,15\)). Memorising triples makes distance calculations much faster!

❓ Pengenalan Masalah Problem Introduction

Masalah: Titik \(A(1,2)\) dan \(B(7,10)\) terletak pada bidang koordinat. Ruas garis \(AB\) miring, sehingga kita tidak bisa menghitung kotaknya secara langsung. Berapa panjang \(AB\)?Problem: Points \(A(1,2)\) and \(B(7,10)\) lie on a coordinate plane. Segment \(AB\) is slanted, so we cannot simply count the squares. How long is \(AB\)?

🔍 Membangun Segitiga Siku-siku Building a Right Triangle

  1. Gambar titik \(A(1,2)\) dan \(B(7,10)\).Plot points \(A(1,2)\) and \(B(7,10)\).
  2. Tarik garis mendatar dari \(A\) dan garis tegak dari \(B\). Keduanya bertemu di titik sudut siku-siku \(C(7,2)\) — ambil \(x\) dari \(B\) dan \(y\) dari \(A\).Draw a horizontal line from \(A\) and a vertical line from \(B\). They meet at the right-angle corner \(C(7,2)\) — take \(x\) from \(B\) and \(y\) from \(A\).
  3. Hitung panjang sisi siku-siku dengan selisih koordinat:Find the leg lengths using coordinate differences: \[ AC = 7-1 = 6 \qquad BC = 10-2 = 8 \]
  4. Terapkan Teorema Pythagoras:Apply the Pythagorean Theorem: \[ AB^2 = 6^2+8^2 = 36+64 = 100 \]
  5. Tarik akar: \(AB=\sqrt{100}=10\) satuan.Take the root: \(AB=\sqrt{100}=10\) units.

✨ Dari Contoh ke Rumus Umum From the Example to the General Formula

Ganti angka dengan huruf: \(A(x_1,y_1)\) dan \(B(x_2,y_2)\). Titik sudut siku-siku \(C(x_2,y_1)\).Replace the numbers with letters: \(A(x_1,y_1)\) and \(B(x_2,y_2)\). The right-angle corner is \(C(x_2,y_1)\).

\[ \underbrace{\Delta x = x_2-x_1}_{\text{selisih mendatar / horizontal change}} \qquad \underbrace{\Delta y = y_2-y_1}_{\text{selisih tegak / vertical change}} \]
Rumus Jarak / The Distance Formula \[ d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2} \quad\text{atau / or}\quad d=\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2} \]

📌 Mengapa dikuadratkan? Selisih bisa negatif (misalnya \(-8\)), tetapi panjang tidak mungkin negatif. Karena \((-8)^2=8^2=64\), kuadrat menghapus tanda negatif. Akibatnya, urutan titik tidak berpengaruh: \((x_2-x_1)^2=(x_1-x_2)^2\).📌 Why square? A difference can be negative (for example \(-8\)), but a length cannot be negative. Because \((-8)^2=8^2=64\), squaring removes the negative sign. As a result, the order of the points does not matter: \((x_2-x_1)^2=(x_1-x_2)^2\).

Contoh 2 / Example 2: \(P(-2,1)\) dan / and \(Q(4,-7)\)
Cara 1 (dari P ke Q):Method 1 (from P to Q): \[ \Delta x = 4-(-2) = 6,\quad \Delta y = -7-1 = -8,\quad d=\sqrt{6^2+(-8)^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=\sqrt{100}=10 \] Cara 2 (dari Q ke P) — hasilnya sama:Method 2 (from Q to P) — same result: \[ \Delta x = -2-4 = -6,\quad \Delta y = 1-(-7) = 8,\quad d=\sqrt{36+64}=10 \]

🤝 Latihan Terbimbing Guided Practice

Kita akan memakai 4 langkah tetap untuk setiap soal jarak. Ikuti langkah ini dengan teliti, terutama saat ada bilangan negatif.We will use 4 fixed steps for every distance problem. Follow them carefully, especially when there are negative numbers.

🪜 Metode 4 Langkah The 4-Step Method

  1. Label titik: \((x_1,y_1)\) dan \((x_2,y_2)\).Label the points: \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\).
  2. Hitung selisih: \(\Delta x=x_2-x_1\) dan \(\Delta y=y_2-y_1\). Tulis kurung untuk bilangan negatif!Find the differences: \(\Delta x=x_2-x_1\) and \(\Delta y=y_2-y_1\). Use brackets for negative numbers!
  3. Kuadratkan dan jumlahkan: \((\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2\).Square and add: \((\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2\).
  4. Tarik akar dan sederhanakan bila perlu.Take the square root and simplify if needed.

🧮 Contoh Soal Terbimbing Guided Worked Examples

Contoh A: koordinat negatif / Example A: negative coordinates
Jarak \(C(-3,4)\) ke \(D(5,-2)\).Distance from \(C(-3,4)\) to \(D(5,-2)\). \[\begin{aligned} \Delta x &= 5-(-3)=8\\ \Delta y &= -2-4=-6\\ d^2 &= 8^2+(-6)^2=64+36=100\\ d &= \sqrt{100}=10 \end{aligned}\]
Contoh B: jawaban bentuk akar / Example B: surd answer
Jarak \(E(1,-1)\) ke \(F(4,5)\).Distance from \(E(1,-1)\) to \(F(4,5)\). \[\begin{aligned} \Delta x &= 4-1=3, \quad \Delta y = 5-(-1)=6\\ d &= \sqrt{3^2+6^2}=\sqrt{9+36}=\sqrt{45}=\sqrt{9\times5}=3\sqrt5\approx 6{,}71 \end{aligned}\]
Contoh C: garis mendatar / Example C: horizontal line
\(G(-4,3)\) dan \(H(6,3)\) punya nilai \(y\) sama, jadi \(\Delta y=0\).\(G(-4,3)\) and \(H(6,3)\) have the same \(y\)-value, so \(\Delta y=0\). \[ d=\sqrt{(6-(-4))^2+0^2}=\sqrt{100}=10 \]
Contoh D: dari titik asal / Example D: from the origin
Jarak \(O(0,0)\) ke \(P(x,y)\) adalah \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\). Contoh \(P(-5,12)\):The distance from \(O(0,0)\) to \(P(x,y)\) is \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\). Example \(P(-5,12)\): \[ d=\sqrt{(-5)^2+12^2}=\sqrt{25+144}=\sqrt{169}=13 \]

🧠 Latihan Mandiri Independent Practice

Sekarang kita gunakan rumus jarak untuk masalah geometri: keliling, jenis segitiga, koordinat yang hilang, dan membandingkan jarak.Now we use the distance formula for geometry problems: perimeter, types of triangle, missing coordinates, and comparing distances.

A. Keliling Bangun dari Koordinat A. Perimeter from Coordinates

Hitung panjang setiap sisi dengan rumus jarak, lalu jumlahkan.Find each side length with the distance formula, then add them.

Contoh / Example: \(A(0,0),\ B(6,0),\ C(6,8)\)
\[ AB = 6-0 = 6,\quad BC = 8-0 = 8,\quad AC=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{100}=10 \] \[ K = AB+BC+AC = 6+8+10 = 24 \text{ satuan / units} \]

B. Menentukan Jenis Segitiga B. Classifying a Triangle

  • Sama kaki: dua sisi sama panjang.Isosceles: two equal sides.
  • Sama sisi: ketiga sisi sama panjang.Equilateral: all three sides equal.
  • Siku-siku: kuadrat sisi terpanjang = jumlah kuadrat dua sisi lain.Right: square of the longest side = sum of squares of the other two.
Contoh / Example: \(P(1,1),\ Q(4,5),\ R(8,2)\)
\[\begin{aligned} PQ&=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=5\\ QR&=\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}=\sqrt{25}=5\\ PR&=\sqrt{7^2+1^2}=\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt2 \end{aligned}\] \(PQ=QR\) → sama kaki. Cek: \(PQ^2+QR^2=25+25=50=PR^2\) → siku-siku. Jadi segitiga siku-siku sama kaki.\(PQ=QR\) → isosceles. Check: \(PQ^2+QR^2=25+25=50=PR^2\) → right-angled. So it is a right isosceles triangle.

C. Mencari Koordinat yang Hilang C. Finding a Missing Coordinate

Masukkan data ke rumus jarak, kuadratkan kedua ruas, lalu selesaikan persamaannya. Biasanya ada dua jawaban (\(\pm\)).Substitute into the distance formula, square both sides, then solve. There are usually two answers (\(\pm\)).

Contoh / Example: \(A(2,3),\ B(x,7),\ AB=5\)
\[\begin{aligned} \sqrt{(x-2)^2+(7-3)^2}&=5\\ (x-2)^2+16&=25\\ (x-2)^2&=9\\ x-2&=\pm 3\\ x=5 \ \text{atau/or}\ x&=-1 \end{aligned}\]

D. Membandingkan Jarak D. Comparing Distances

Untuk mengurutkan jarak, cukup bandingkan \(d^2\) (tidak perlu menarik akar), karena jika \(d_1^2 \lt d_2^2\) maka \(d_1 \lt d_2\).To order distances, just compare \(d^2\) (no need to take roots), because if \(d_1^2 \lt d_2^2\) then \(d_1 \lt d_2\).

Contoh / Example
Dari \(O(0,0)\): \(M(2,4)\to d^2=4+16=20\); \(N(-3,3)\to d^2=9+9=18\). Jadi \(N\) lebih dekat ke \(O\) daripada \(M\).From \(O(0,0)\): \(M(2,4)\to d^2=4+16=20\); \(N(-3,3)\to d^2=9+9=18\). So \(N\) is closer to \(O\) than \(M\).
