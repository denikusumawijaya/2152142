🔢A. Kuadrat Suatu BilanganA. Squaring a Number

Kuadrat suatu bilangan adalah hasil kali bilangan itu dengan dirinya sendiri.The square of a number is the product of that number with itself.

Rumus · Formula
$$a^2 = a \times a$$
Contoh 1Example 1
$$13^2 = 13 \times 13 = 169 \qquad (1{,}5)^2 = 1{,}5 \times 1{,}5 = 2{,}25 \qquad (2{,}4)^2 = 5{,}76$$

⚠️ Jangan tertukar: $13^2 \ne 13 \times 2$. Kuadrat bukan dikali 2!⚠️ Don't mix them up: $13^2 \ne 13 \times 2$. Squaring is not multiplying by 2!

√B. Akar KuadratB. Square Roots

Akar kuadrat adalah kebalikan dari kuadrat. Kita mencari bilangan positif yang jika dikuadratkan menghasilkan bilangan di dalam akar.The square root is the inverse of squaring. We look for the positive number that, when squared, gives the number inside the root.

Rumus · Formula
$$\sqrt{b} = a \iff a^2 = b, \quad a \ge 0$$
Contoh 2: Tentukan $\sqrt{225}$Example 2: Find $\sqrt{225}$
  1. Cari bilangan yang kuadratnya $225$. Angka terakhir $5$, jadi akarnya berakhiran $5$.Look for the number whose square is $225$. The last digit is $5$, so the root ends in $5$.
  2. Coba $15$: $15^2 = 15 \times 15 = 225$ ✔️Try $15$: $15^2 = 15 \times 15 = 225$ ✔️
  3. Jadi, $\sqrt{225} = 15$.So, $\sqrt{225} = 15$.

Dalam soal panjang sisi, persamaan $x^2 = 144$ memberi $x = 12$ (kita ambil yang positif karena panjang tidak mungkin negatif).In side-length problems, the equation $x^2 = 144$ gives $x = 12$ (we take the positive value because a length cannot be negative).

✂️C. Menyederhanakan Bentuk AkarC. Simplifying Square Roots

Sifat · Property
$$\sqrt{a \times b} = \sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b}$$
Contoh 3: Sederhanakan $\sqrt{75}$Example 3: Simplify $\sqrt{75}$
  1. Faktorkan $75$ menjadi (kuadrat sempurna terbesar) × (sisa): $75 = 25 \times 3$.Factor $75$ into (largest perfect square) × (remainder): $75 = 25 \times 3$.
  2. Pisahkan akarnya: $\sqrt{75} = \sqrt{25} \times \sqrt{3}$.Split the root: $\sqrt{75} = \sqrt{25} \times \sqrt{3}$.
  3. Hitung $\sqrt{25} = 5$, jadi $\sqrt{75} = 5\sqrt{3}$.Compute $\sqrt{25} = 5$, so $\sqrt{75} = 5\sqrt{3}$.
$$\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \times 2} = 6\sqrt{2} \qquad \sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt{2} \qquad \sqrt{48} = \sqrt{16\times 3} = 4\sqrt{3}$$

🚫D. Kesalahan yang Sering TerjadiD. A Common Mistake

Akar dari penjumlahan tidak sama dengan penjumlahan akar.The root of a sum is not equal to the sum of the roots.

$$\sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \qquad \text{tetapi (but)} \qquad \sqrt{9} + \sqrt{16} = 3 + 4 = 7$$

Jadi $\sqrt{9+16} \ne \sqrt{9} + \sqrt{16}$. Selalu jumlahkan dulu, baru tarik akar.So $\sqrt{9+16} \ne \sqrt{9} + \sqrt{16}$. Always add first, then take the root.

⚖️E. Membandingkan Bentuk AkarE. Comparing Square Roots

Ubah semua bilangan ke bentuk $\sqrt{\ \ }$, lalu bandingkan bilangan di dalam akar.Rewrite every number in the form $\sqrt{\ \ }$, then compare the numbers inside the roots.

$$a\sqrt{b} = \sqrt{a^2 \times b} \qquad\qquad a = \sqrt{a^2}$$
Contoh 4: Urutkan $5\sqrt{3}$, $\sqrt{70}$, dan $8$ dari yang terkecil.Example 4: Order $5\sqrt{3}$, $\sqrt{70}$ and $8$ from smallest.
  1. $5\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{25 \times 3} = \sqrt{75}$
  2. $8 = \sqrt{64}$
  3. Bandingkan: $64 \lt 70 \lt 75$, maka urutannya $8,\ \sqrt{70},\ 5\sqrt{3}$.Compare: $64 \lt 70 \lt 75$, so the order is $8,\ \sqrt{70},\ 5\sqrt{3}$.

🏛️Siapa Pythagoras?Who was Pythagoras?

Pythagoras adalah matematikawan Yunani kuno (sekitar 570–495 SM). Teorema yang memakai namanya menghubungkan ketiga sisi segitiga siku-siku. Bangsa Babilonia, Mesir, India, dan Tiongkok juga sudah memakai hubungan ini untuk membangun dan mengukur tanah.Pythagoras was an ancient Greek mathematician (about 570–495 BC). The theorem named after him links the three sides of a right triangle. The Babylonians, Egyptians, Indians and Chinese also used this relationship for building and measuring land.

$$c^2 = a^2 + b^2$$
$c$ = sisi miring (hypotenuse), $a, b$ = sisi siku-siku (legs)

📱Contoh 1: Diagonal Layar PonselExample 1: Phone Screen Diagonal

Layar ponsel berukuran lebar $6$ cm dan tinggi $8$ cm. Berapa panjang diagonalnya?A phone screen is $6$ cm wide and $8$ cm tall. How long is its diagonal?

  1. Lebar dan tinggi saling tegak lurus → sisi siku-siku. Diagonal $d$ → sisi miring.Width and height are perpendicular → legs. Diagonal $d$ → hypotenuse.
  2. $d^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100$
  3. $d = \sqrt{100} = 10$ cm.

🏗️Contoh 2: Aturan 3-4-5 Tukang BangunanExample 2: The Builders' 3-4-5 Rule

Tukang menandai $3$ m pada satu sisi dan $4$ m pada sisi lainnya. Jika jarak kedua tanda tepat $5$ m, sudutnya pasti siku-siku, sebab:A builder marks $3$ m on one side and $4$ m on the other. If the distance between the marks is exactly $5$ m, the corner must be a right angle, because:

$$3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$$

Kelipatannya juga berlaku, misalnya $6$ m, $8$ m, $10$ m.Multiples also work, for example $6$ m, $8$ m, $10$ m.

⚽Contoh 3: Jalan Pintas LapanganExample 3: Shortcut Across a Field

Lapangan berbentuk persegi panjang $40$ m × $30$ m. Berapa meter yang dihemat jika berjalan lewat diagonal dibandingkan menyusuri dua sisinya?A rectangular field measures $40$ m × $30$ m. How many metres are saved by walking along the diagonal instead of along two sides?

  1. Menyusuri tepi: $40 + 30 = 70$ m.Walking along the edges: $40 + 30 = 70$ m.
  2. Diagonal: $d = \sqrt{40^2 + 30^2} = \sqrt{1600 + 900} = \sqrt{2500} = 50$ m.Diagonal: $d = \sqrt{40^2 + 30^2} = \sqrt{1600 + 900} = \sqrt{2500} = 50$ m.
  3. Hemat: $70 - 50 = 20$ m.Saved: $70 - 50 = 20$ m.
💡 Kunci: Cari sudut siku-siku dulu. Sisi yang berhadapan dengan sudut siku-siku adalah sisi miring (sisi terpanjang).💡 Key: Find the right angle first. The side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse (the longest side).

Mengulang Pengetahuan PrasyaratReview of Prior Knowledge

📐1. Bunyi Teorema Pythagoras1. Statement of the Pythagorean Theorem

Pada segitiga siku-siku, kuadrat sisi miring sama dengan jumlah kuadrat kedua sisi siku-sikunya.In a right triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the two legs.

Tiga bentuk rumus · Three forms of the formula
$$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 \qquad a^2 = c^2 - b^2 \qquad b^2 = c^2 - a^2$$

➕ Mencari sisi miring → jumlahkan kuadrat. ➖ Mencari sisi siku-siku → kurangkan kuadrat.➕ Finding the hypotenuse → add the squares. ➖ Finding a leg → subtract the squares.

🧮2. Mencari Sisi yang Belum Diketahui2. Finding an Unknown Side

Contoh 1: sisi siku-siku $8$ cm dan $15$ cm. Tentukan sisi miring $c$.Example 1: legs $8$ cm and $15$ cm. Find the hypotenuse $c$.
$$c^2 = 8^2 + 15^2 = 64 + 225 = 289 \;\Rightarrow\; c = \sqrt{289} = 17 \text{ cm}$$
Contoh 2: sisi miring $25$ cm, satu sisi siku-siku $7$ cm. Tentukan sisi lainnya $b$.Example 2: hypotenuse $25$ cm, one leg $7$ cm. Find the other leg $b$.
$$b^2 = 25^2 - 7^2 = 625 - 49 = 576 \;\Rightarrow\; b = \sqrt{576} = 24 \text{ cm}$$

🔢3. Tripel Pythagoras3. Pythagorean Triples

Tiga bilangan bulat positif $(a, b, c)$ disebut tripel Pythagoras jika $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Kelipatannya juga tripel.Three positive whole numbers $(a, b, c)$ are a Pythagorean triple if $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Their multiples are also triples.

🔺4. Menentukan Jenis Segitiga (Kebalikan Teorema Pythagoras)4. Determining the Type of Triangle (Converse of the Theorem)

Misalkan $c$ adalah sisi terpanjang. Bandingkan $c^2$ dengan $a^2 + b^2$:Let $c$ be the longest side. Compare $c^2$ with $a^2 + b^2$:

Kondisi · ConditionJenis segitiga · Type of triangle
$c^2 = a^2 + b^2$Siku-siku Right
$c^2 \lt a^2 + b^2$Lancip Acute
$c^2 \gt a^2 + b^2$Tumpul Obtuse
Contoh 3Example 3
  1. Sisi $6, 7, 9$: $9^2 = 81$ dan $6^2 + 7^2 = 36 + 49 = 85$. Karena $81 \lt 85$ → lancip.Sides $6, 7, 9$: $9^2 = 81$ and $6^2 + 7^2 = 85$. Since $81 \lt 85$ → acute.
  2. Sisi $5, 6, 8$: $8^2 = 64$ dan $5^2 + 6^2 = 25 + 36 = 61$. Karena $64 \gt 61$ → tumpul.Sides $5, 6, 8$: $8^2 = 64$ and $5^2 + 6^2 = 61$. Since $64 \gt 61$ → obtuse.
  3. Sisi $8, 15, 17$: $17^2 = 289$ dan $64 + 225 = 289$ → siku-siku.Sides $8, 15, 17$: $17^2 = 289$ and $64 + 225 = 289$ → right.

⭐5. Segitiga Siku-siku Istimewa5. Special Right Triangles

Segitiga $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$The $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle

$$\text{sisi siku} : \text{sisi siku} : \text{sisi miring} = 1 : 1 : \sqrt{2}$$

Contoh: sisi siku-siku $5$ cm → sisi miring $5\sqrt{2}$ cm (karena $\sqrt{5^2+5^2} = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt2$).Example: legs $5$ cm → hypotenuse $5\sqrt{2}$ cm (because $\sqrt{5^2+5^2} = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt2$).

Segitiga $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$The $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle

$$\text{depan } 30^\circ : \text{depan } 60^\circ : \text{sisi miring} = 1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$$

Contoh: sisi miring $10$ cm → sisi di depan $30^\circ$ $= 5$ cm, sisi di depan $60^\circ$ $= 5\sqrt{3}$ cm.Example: hypotenuse $10$ cm → side opposite $30^\circ$ $= 5$ cm, side opposite $60^\circ$ $= 5\sqrt{3}$ cm.

🪜Masalah Utama: Tangga Petugas PLNMain Problem: The Electrician's Ladder

Seorang petugas menyandarkan tangga sepanjang $5$ m pada dinding tegak. Kaki tangga berjarak $1{,}4$ m dari dinding. Setinggi apa ujung atas tangga menyentuh dinding?A worker leans a $5$ m ladder against a vertical wall. The foot of the ladder is $1{,}4$ m from the wall. How high up the wall does the top of the ladder reach?

🗺️Strategi 5 Langkah Menyelesaikan Soal Cerita5-Step Strategy for Word Problems

  1. Pahami masalah: tulis yang diketahui dan ditanya.Understand the problem: write what is known and what is asked.
    Diketahui: tangga $= 5$ m, jarak kaki $= 1{,}4$ m. Ditanya: tinggi $t$.Known: ladder $= 5$ m, distance of foot $= 1{,}4$ m. Asked: height $t$.
  2. Gambar sketsa segitiga siku-siku dan tandai sudut siku-siku (antara dinding dan lantai).Draw a sketch of the right triangle and mark the right angle (between the wall and the floor).
  3. Tentukan sisi miring lalu tulis persamaan. Tangga berhadapan dengan sudut siku-siku → sisi miring. Yang dicari sisi siku-siku → kurangkan.Identify the hypotenuse, then write the equation. The ladder is opposite the right angle → hypotenuse. We want a leg → subtract.
    $$t^2 = 5^2 - (1{,}4)^2$$
  4. Substitusi dan hitung.Substitute and calculate.
    $$t^2 = 25 - 1{,}96 = 23{,}04 \;\Rightarrow\; t = \sqrt{23{,}04} = 4{,}8$$
  5. Periksa & simpulkan dengan satuan: $4{,}8 \lt 5$ (sisi siku-siku harus lebih pendek dari sisi miring) ✔️. Jadi tangga mencapai ketinggian $4{,}8$ m.Check & conclude with units: $4{,}8 \lt 5$ (a leg must be shorter than the hypotenuse) ✔️. So the ladder reaches a height of $4{,}8$ m.

🚢Contoh Kedua: Kapal NelayanSecond Example: A Fishing Boat

Kapal berlayar $6$ km ke utara, lalu $8$ km ke timur. Berapa jarak kapal dari pelabuhan?A boat sails $6$ km north, then $8$ km east. How far is the boat from the harbour?

  1. Utara ⟂ timur, jadi $6$ dan $8$ adalah sisi siku-siku; jarak $d$ adalah sisi miring → jumlahkan.North ⟂ east, so $6$ and $8$ are legs; distance $d$ is the hypotenuse → add.
  2. $d^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100$
  3. $d = \sqrt{100} = 10$ km.

🚩Contoh Ketiga: Tali Tiang BenderaThird Example: Flagpole Rope

$$x^2 = 13^2 - 12^2 = 169 - 144 = 25 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 5 \text{ m}$$

  2. Segitiga kecil: sisi miring $13$, sisi siku-siku $5$ dan $t$.Small triangle: hypotenuse $13$, legs $5$ and $t$.
  3. $t = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ cm.

📦Contoh 5: Diagonal Ruang BalokExample 5: Space Diagonal of a Cuboid

Balok berukuran $p = 12$ cm, $l = 4$ cm, $t = 3$ cm. Tentukan diagonal ruangnya.A cuboid has $p = 12$ cm, $l = 4$ cm, $t = 3$ cm. Find its space diagonal.

  1. Diagonal alas (pakai Pythagoras pertama): $d_b^2 = 12^2 + 4^2 = 144 + 16 = 160$.Base diagonal (first Pythagoras): $d_b^2 = 12^2 + 4^2 = 160$.
  2. Diagonal ruang (Pythagoras kedua, $d_b \perp t$): $d_r^2 = d_b^2 + t^2 = 160 + 9 = 169$.Space diagonal (second Pythagoras, $d_b \perp t$): $d_r^2 = 160 + 9 = 169$.
  3. $d_r = \sqrt{169} = 13$ cm.
Rumus singkat · Shortcut
$$d_r = \sqrt{p^2 + l^2 + t^2}$$

  3. Cek: $4^2 + (4\sqrt3)^2 = 16 + 48 = 64 = 8^2$ ✔️Check: $4^2 + (4\sqrt3)^2 = 16 + 48 = 64 = 8^2$ ✔️

🪁Contoh D: Tinggi dari Tanah (Ditambah Tinggi Tangan)Example D: Height Above the Ground (Adding the Hand Height)

Benang $50$ m, jarak mendatar $30$ m, tangan berada $1{,}5$ m di atas tanah. Tinggi di atas tangan $= \sqrt{50^2 - 30^2} = 40$ m. Tinggi dari tanah $= 40 + 1{,}5 = 41{,}5$ m.String $50$ m, horizontal distance $30$ m, the hand is $1{,}5$ m above the ground. Height above the hand $= 40$ m. Height above the ground $= 40 + 1{,}5 = 41{,}5$ m.

⚖️Contoh E: Membandingkan Panjang Tanpa KalkulatorExample E: Comparing Lengths Without a Calculator

Mana yang lebih panjang: diagonal persegi bersisi $6$ atau sisi miring segitiga dengan sisi siku-siku $5$ dan $7$? Bandingkan kuadratnya: $6^2 + 6^2 = 72$ dan $5^2 + 7^2 = 74$. Karena $72 \lt 74$, sisi miring segitiga lebih panjang.Which is longer: the diagonal of a square with side $6$, or the hypotenuse of a triangle with legs $5$ and $7$? Compare their squares: $72$ and $74$. Since $72 \lt 74$, the triangle's hypotenuse is longer.

⭐Poin PentingKey Points

  1. Teorema Pythagoras hanya berlaku untuk segitiga siku-siku: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$, dengan $c$ sisi miring.The Pythagorean Theorem only works for right triangles: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$, where $c$ is the hypotenuse.
  2. Mencari sisi miring → jumlahkan kuadrat; mencari sisi siku-siku → kurangkan kuadrat.Finding the hypotenuse → add the squares; finding a leg → subtract the squares.
  3. Dalam soal cerita: cari sudut siku-siku, gambar sketsa, tentukan sisi miring, hitung, lalu tulis satuan.In word problems: find the right angle, sketch, identify the hypotenuse, calculate, then write the units.
  4. Jenis segitiga: $c^2 = a^2+b^2$ siku-siku, $c^2 \lt a^2+b^2$ lancip, $c^2 \gt a^2+b^2$ tumpul.Triangle type: $c^2 = a^2+b^2$ right, $c^2 \lt a^2+b^2$ acute, $c^2 \gt a^2+b^2$ obtuse.
  5. Segitiga istimewa: $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$ → $1 : 1 : \sqrt2$; $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ → $1 : \sqrt3 : 2$.Special triangles: $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$ → $1 : 1 : \sqrt2$; $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ → $1 : \sqrt3 : 2$.
  6. Jarak dua titik: $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$; diagonal ruang balok: $d = \sqrt{p^2 + l^2 + t^2}$.Distance between two points: $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$; space diagonal of a cuboid: $d = \sqrt{p^2 + l^2 + t^2}$.
